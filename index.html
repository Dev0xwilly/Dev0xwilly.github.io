To minimize the risk of enforcement, ensure your account is active and authentic, has a profile photo and display name, has a verified phone number or email (where required), and does not engage in spammy or suspicious behavior.

How to Keep Your X Account Safe
  1. Avoid spamming replies, mentions, direct messages, or repetitive posts.
  2. Do not use bots, fake engagement, engagement groups, or services that artificially inflate likes, reposts, followers, or replies.
  3. Do not impersonate another individual, brand, organization, or public figure.
  4. Do not use false, misleading, stolen, or deceptive profile information.
  5. Do not post threats, harassment, hateful content, or abusive material.
  6. Respect people's privacy—do not share another person's private information without permission.
  7. Do not promote illegal, restricted, or harmful products or services.
  8. Do not use hacked, compromised, or suspicious accounts.
  9. Avoid making multiple profile changes immediately after subscribing—especially your username, display name, profile photo, or bio.
  10. Keep your email address and phone number verified whenever possible.
